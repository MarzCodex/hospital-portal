1. Introduction

Welcome to the Hospital Service Portal

The Hospital Service Portal is a comprehensive healthcare management system designed to streamline patient care, insurance verification, and administrative processes. Built with modern web technologies, it provides a unified platform for managing all aspects of hospital operations.

Key Features

Member Verification
Service Approvals
Inpatient Management
Referral Processing
Claims Management
User & Role Management

System Overview

The system is built with a modular architecture, allowing healthcare facilities to manage:

  • Patient registration and verification
  • Insurance and benefit management
  • Inpatient and outpatient services
  • Referral and claims processing
  • User access control and permissions

2. Getting Started

Logging In

To access the Hospital Service Portal, users must have valid credentials provided by the system administrator.

  • Navigate to the login page
  • Enter your username or hospital number
  • Enter your password
  • Click the Login button

Default Credentials

  • Username: admin
  • Password: admin123

⚠️ Change default passwords immediately after first login.

Dashboard Overview

After logging in, you will be directed to the Dashboard, which provides quick access to all system modules through interactive cards.

3. User Management

Overview

The User Management module allows administrators to create, edit, and manage system users.

Key Functions

  • Add User: Create new user accounts with unique usernames and roles
  • Edit User: Update user information and permissions
  • Reset Password: Reset user passwords to default (HPU-2026)
  • Toggle Status: Activate or deactivate user accounts
  • Delete User: Permanently remove users from the system

User Roles

  • System Administrator: Full system access
  • Doctor: Clinical access to patient records
  • Front Desk: Patient registration and verification
  • Data Clerk: Data entry and reporting

4. Outpatient Services

Member Verification

Verify patient eligibility and coverage by searching for their authorization number.

  • Enter the authorization number
  • View patient profile and benefit details
  • Authorize services for the patient

Service Approvals

Manage service approval requests through four tabs:

  • Request Approval: Submit new approval requests
  • Requested Approval: View pending approvals
  • Issuing Service: Issue approved services
  • Issued Approval Services: View completed issuances

5. Inpatient Services

Patient Admission

Admit patients to the hospital by searching for their authorization number.

  • Search by authorization number
  • Auto-populate patient information
  • Select ward type and room type
  • Enter admission details and reason

Discharge & Transfer

Manage patient discharge with a complete form.

  • View admitted patients list
  • Click discharge to open the discharge form
  • Enter discharge details, diagnosis, and remarks
  • Confirm and complete the discharge

Admitted Patients List

View all admitted and discharged patients with status filters and export capabilities.

10. FAQs & Troubleshooting

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: How do I reset my password?

A: Click on your user avatar, select "Change Password," and follow the prompts.


Q: How do I verify a patient?

A: Go to Outpatient Services → Member Verification, enter the authorization number, and click Search.


Q: How do I discharge a patient?

A: Go to Inpatient Services → Discharge/Transfer, find the patient, and click the "Discharge" button.


Common Issues

  • Login Failed: Check username/password, ensure account is active
  • Patient Not Found: Verify the authorization number is correct
  • Permissions Error: Contact your system administrator

📌 For additional support, contact the ICT-UNIT.
